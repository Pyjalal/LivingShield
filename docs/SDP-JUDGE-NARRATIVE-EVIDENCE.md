## What the official brief actually rewards

The public programme chooses one People/Planet track, includes climate resilience within Planet, and asks pitches to explain the beneficiary/value mechanism separately from the technology enabling implementation. It explicitly considers practical township pilots. The official weights are below; the third column is our interpretation of the demonstration burden, not additional organiser criteria. [Official programme and FAQ](https://www.simedarbyproperty.com/universityhackathon/).

| Criterion, shortened | Weight | What LivingShield should prove in the pitch |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Strategic/thematic fit | 15% | Name an SDP user, a real township and a specific decision. |
| Impact and sustainability | 25% | Define a measurable operator pilot and the resident/environmental purpose. |
| Originality | 20% | Explain the new integration and credit existing engines and data. |
| Practical delivery | 20% | Show the working decision path and what remains for professional review. |
| Commercial/scaling potential | 10% | Explain the repeatable option-review workflow across qualified source packs. |
| Team, evidence and pitch | 10% | Demonstrate actual results and complete the team/contribution record. |

The rubric therefore gives **70%** to fit, impact, feasibility and commercial multiplication combined. A tour of attractive tools cannot establish those outcomes. The public page was checked for the criteria and pitch requirements; no private participant instructions were available.

## Recommended positioning

**Headline:** Before SDP commits, test the flood idea.

**One-sentence proposition:** LivingShield helps a township asset team compare local storage concepts at Bandar Bukit Raja and leave with a source-linked investigation brief an engineer can review.

**Named user:** a proposed SDP township asset/development manager working with a drainage engineer. This is an intended user persona, not a verified internal job title or interviewed customer.

**Job to be done:** when an intervention is proposed, make its assumptions and next investigation explicit before deciding what deserves further site work. The prototype explores a concept under declared assumptions; it does not rank actual capital projects or certify a scheme.

**Decision/output:** which question to take to the next engineering review, with a proposed reviewer, next action, requested site evidence and a downloadable brief. Prepared remains local authorship, not a work order, acceptance or approval.

This position is a proposed complement to established professional processes. There is no evidence that SDP's existing tools are inadequate, that this replaces Swiss Re or consultants, or that an operator will adopt it.

## Three-minute story that the current product can support

| Time | Judge's question | Show and say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:25 | Why should SDP care? | Name the asset manager and the pending investigation decision. Use one current company source, then open Bandar Bukit Raja immediately. |
| 0:25–1:20 | What decision changes? | Declare the same assumed storm. Compute the baseline, add local storage and compare the same time. Ask whether the concepts actually receive water and use their capacity. Show real capture/unused capacity; do not give the judge a fixed protection percentage. |
| 1:20–1:50 | Why is this more than animation? | Open the result explanation: actual local capture, inlet/capacity assumptions and water accounting. Point out a missing site fact that could change the investigation. |
| 1:50–2:25 | What does the manager leave with? | Prepare the local review, name the proposed role and next action, request the required survey/observations, and export the readable brief. Make the output inspectable. |
| 2:25–3:00 | What can SDP pilot? | Ask for one live option-review task, an asset/engineering sponsor and an approved source pack. Compare briefing time, unaided task completion and reviewer-assessed completeness against the existing process. Report targets as proposed, not achieved. |

The current bank module should be an **optional design-investigation branch** after the core storage story. If shown, explain the different engineering question and export its declared quantities. Forcing the bank film into the principal flood-comparison sequence creates an apparent causal connection the model does not establish.

## Claims and shortcuts to reject

- Do not say the application identifies the best actual SDP mitigation scheme, reconstructs a historical flood or predicts the next monsoon.
- The three 225 ha windows are analyst pilot windows, not entire townships, surveyed catchments or SDP ownership boundaries. Elmina is a design comparator, not proof of inundated homes.
- The current solver omits installed pipe/drain networks, tides, river/external inflows and individual building barriers. It cannot evaluate the disclosed pump/gate programme or quantify the benefit of desilting.
- Never convert grid wet area into homes protected, resident evacuations, safe road access or financial losses avoided.
- The physical bank modules receive no storage or flood-reduction credit. Quantities and an illustrative planted scene do not prove stability, ecology, installed cost or suitability at a real bank.
- A visually dramatic change is not calibration. Use the actual computed case and label its assumptions; obtain engineering evidence before making performance claims.
- The operator pilot is proposed. No measured workflow saving, customer interview, buyer commitment or SDP endorsement is established.
