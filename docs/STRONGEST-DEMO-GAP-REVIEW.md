## Three concrete visual improvements

1. **Let the scene lead during presentation.** Provide an explicit, reversible Present view / expanded scene that compacts the large introduction and secondary planning chrome while keeping pilot, mode, comparison time, limitations and exit reachable. Compact the large corner headline on mobile. This closes the difference shown by the initial LivingShield scene bounds versus Solar Kita/TamanKu's immediate scene presence. Do not enlarge buildings artificially or imply a surveyed bank location.
2. **Move the guide to the actual task.** When Compare the storm is chosen, reveal and focus or scroll to the real FloodLab controls; provide a visible jump to the storm controls from the scene. The initial guide changed its instructions while the action remained below the viewport. Preserve the same native computation and comparison rather than substituting a canned percentage or screenshot. A focus/jump target should also work with reduced motion and keyboard navigation.
3. **Make the guided story the landing's primary entry.** Promote Start the guided demo to the main hero action while preserving direct access to the twin and module. The goal of the opening is to demonstrate the connected place → storm → design → evidence sequence; the small initial text link understated it. This is a hierarchy change, not a new feature or a claim of automatic task completion.

These priorities are deliberately separate from the parallel result-specific explanation/per-storage breakdown and local review-plan/export work. Those feature improvements address Surplus AI's inspectable reasoning and TamanKu/NEXORA's understandable next step; they should not be replaced with decorative summaries.
