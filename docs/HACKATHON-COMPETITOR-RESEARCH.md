# Public hackathon competitor research

Checked 21 October 2026 (Malaysia time). Primary-source research complements Browser Agent/Jev visual runs. Repository statements are maintainer claims, not verified functionality or organiser-confirmed entry acceptance. Public search cannot cover private or unindexed projects.

## Official brief

The [current organiser page](https://www.simedarbyproperty.com/universityhackathon/) lists registration ending **21 October**, proposals **23 October**, judging **24–28 October**, top ten **28 October**, mentoring **2–6 November**, final submission **11 November**, pitch day **14 November**, and awards **3 December 2026**. It lists no pitch tomorrow (22 October). Closing times are unspecified.

Teams require **3–5 students**, a leader and at least one coder. Malaysian university students and eligible Malaysia-based PR/work/student-visa holders may apply. Choose one track: **People** (wellbeing, safety, inclusion, connection) or **Planet** (water conservation, biodiversity, climate resilience).

Judging: strategic fit **15%**, impact/sustainability **25%**, innovation/differentiation **20%**, feasibility **20%**, commercial value **10%**, team/evidence/pitch **10%**. Explain the mechanism and demonstrate its technology enabler. The [Kolaxus mirror](https://tot.kolaxus.net/) retains older registration/team/award details; prefer the current organiser page for planning.

[Programme terms](https://www.simedarbyproperty.com/universityhackathon/terms-and-conditions/) cover originality/rights (clause 9), assignment (11), Background IP licensing/disclosure (12). Existing components and third-party data need accurate disclosure. Public availability without a licence does not document redistribution permission. This research made no registration, submission or contact.

## Priority comparisons and build decisions

These are product recommendations inferred from repository claims, not visual rankings:

- **TamanKu**: 3D place context and resident actions. Credit existing engine reuse; differentiate through explainable simulation and a connected operator/resident workflow, not ownership of its original interaction.
- **NEXORA**: water/leak/drain issues connected to operator response. Turn signals into reviewed incidents with responsibility and status.
- **SIAGA**: 3D home sensors, residents and technician cases. Incorporate LivingShield natively in shared township/location state, rather than merely linking to another app.
- **Prismatic Grid**: digital twin, anomalies, forecasts and what-if value. Benchmark hierarchy, before/after causality and auditable outputs; do not imitate unsupported accuracy claims.
- **FloodWay / FloodSafe Route / Township Flood System**: river imagery, disruption and reporting. Our coarse uncalibrated grid must not imply validated safe routes or measured property depths.
- **BlueLoop**: predicted pond operation and water reuse. Demonstrate a clear working decision sequence; operational controls remain scenarios without real asset connections and permission.
- **Givlah**: public walkthrough and captioned short video. Its README distinguishes documentation/video from the local interactive prototype; a missing public app is not a failed live user flow. The [sampled recording review](GIVLAH-VIDEO-OBSERVATIONS.md) observes fictional workflow states at nine timestamps, without claiming continuous playback, audio review or live operations.

Build one master brand and landing page with enterable modules. Demonstrate: inspect real township → run declared storm → compare mitigation at the same camera/time → create/review a LivingShield report → show assigned response and evidence. Keep nationwide data and provenance behind progressive disclosure. Label seeded demonstrations and assumptions. Show case transitions, local capture, shared locations and exports, not only static promotional metrics.

For every reachable public demo, Browser Agent should record the same viewport, short task, screenshot and assertions. Compare hierarchy, clarity, interaction, workflow completeness, mobile usability and failure recovery. Record unavailable demos and untested paths. CasSama metadata points to /CasSama/ while its README points to /CasCas/; verify both. Maintainer claims and public-search coverage do not prove exhaustive competitor testing or a guaranteed win.
