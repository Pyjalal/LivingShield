## Recommended pilot 1: Bandar Bukit Raja

[Developer township page](https://simedarbyproperty.com/bandar-bukit-raja/index.html) identifies SDP township and nature-based stormwater/Townpark context. Its [linked map](https://goo.gl/maps/XAGwezz1VzQkN9PMA) resolves to sales-gallery POI latitude 3.0867496 longitude 101.4323721. The implemented 1.5 km pilot is centred at longitude 101.437697, latitude 3.0852015, with WGS84 bbox approximately `[101.4309423,3.0784566,101.4444517,3.0919464]`. It is an analyst locality window, not an ownership boundary or surveyed catchment.

Primary flood evidence: [SDP 2021 sustainability report,p91](https://www.simedarbyproperty.com/sites/default/files/2022-04/SDP-Annual-Report-2021.pdf) explicitly includes affected households in BBR township following end 2021 floods. [SDP 2022 report](https://www.simedarbyproperty.com/sites/default/files/2023-04/SDP_SR%202022_Website.pdf) records relief to victims in Pagoh and BBR after December 2021. These identify township/event, not exact inundated buildings or depth.

[Selangor assembly 2024 answer 110](https://dewan.selangor.gov.my/question/banjir-17/) documents BBR flood-response works, including flapgate/floodwall completion 4 September 2024 and temporary pump testing. [Parliament 5 February 2025,p122](https://www.parlimen.gov.my/files/hindex/pdf/DR-05022025.pdf) discusses 12 November 2024 flooding of routes including Persiaran Sungai Keramat in BBR. This is an official parliamentary statement, not surveyed flood geometry. Do not apply corridor-depth reports to individual properties.

[SDP 2024 report,p75](https://www.simedarbyproperty.com/annualreport2024/download/sdp-sr2024.pdf) describes wetlands/canals and low-lying park retention. [Developer resilience booklet](https://www.simedarbyproperty.com/bandar-bukit-raja/booklet.pdf) is useful precedent; prior source research identifies 62,500 m³ and 3.3 km swales. Verify precise booklet labels before charting; never allocate whole-township storage to the smaller demo catchment. Floodplain function is intentional infrastructure, not proof nearby homes flooded.

## Demo claim discipline

Prefer BBR plus BUP. Mark observed historical events, current observations, design precedent and hypothetical interventions separately. No invented building inundation, annual probability, prevented losses or recurring events. Camera/bbox anchors are navigation values, not exact engineering sites.

The new 3D water overlay is an **uncalibrated surface-flow scenario**, not an observed flood reconstruction. It uses actual Copernicus GLO-30 DSM sampled to 32 × 32 cells across each 225 ha window, with declared rainfall/runoff, roughness, drainage and boundary assumptions. The local-inertial solver routes water, applies wet/dry handling and conserves rainfall/loss/outflow/drainage/storage/surface volume. A 30-minute recession follows rainfall. Before/after shares terrain, storm, camera and time; local mitigation alone differs.

The pooling-point preset places up to twelve 1,500 m³ basins using baseline pooling and a spacing heuristic. Assumed basin inlets are 0.50 m³/s; storage cannot capture water absent from its containing cell or exceed remaining capacity. These suggestions do not establish land availability, inlet connectivity or engineering feasibility. The separate ideal-capture water-budget screen remains available; its editable catchment does not resize the 225 ha flood grid.

The historical DSM includes canopy/roofs and may precede construction. Drains/sewers, culverts, building barriers, river/tidal and external catchment inflow are absent. No observed-event calibration validates parcel depths or homes protected. [Terrain provenance](FLOOD-TERRAIN-SOURCES.md), [numerical verification and reproducible actual-terrain experiments](FLOOD-MODEL-VALIDATION.md), and flood JSON exports support review; numerical conservation is not real-world validation.
