## Evidence standard

Source-only tasks use read-only goals, four or fewer Jev decisions and an expected repository-URL assertion. A repository page or README demonstrates publicly presented source material. It does not demonstrate that an application installs, executes, authenticates, calculates correctly, persists data or completes its advertised workflow. At most two source tasks were queued at once so native application QA and demo retries could share the browser service. No competitor code, assets or accounts were modified.

Completed URL assertions prove location only. Budget exhaustion, blocked navigation, invalid model responses, unavailable hosting and missing product screens remain explicit limitations of their recorded runs. For NEXORA, the initial seven-decision and repeat six-decision Jev attempts both executed zero actions. For Terrarium, the initial and repeat six-decision attempts both executed zero actions. These bounded automation failures do not establish product failure. A later ten-decision NEXORA run executed Explore once and reached sign-in; a later Terrarium Analysis check executed guest entry once and passed the URL assertion while the home modal remained. Givlah’s Jev video run inspected only the opening 0:01 of 3:08. Neither those entries nor one video frame establish a complete feature test.

Later first-hand Playwright checks are recorded separately in the [primary functional/mobile supplement](COMPETITOR-FUNCTIONAL-CHECKS.md) and [secondary functional supplement](COMPETITOR-FUNCTIONAL-SECONDARY.md). The primary report verifies named desktop interactions in eight projects plus eight mobile states. The secondary report covers the other 15 projects and 30 inspected desktop/mobile captures, including later reached AccessTwin and GreenLoop workspaces. Nine secondary projects expose a bounded application flow; the others expose documentation, entry/authentication or hosting/backend boundaries. Most interactive secondary actions occur at 390px before both-width captures; screenshots do not imply every action was replayed at both widths. Together, the reports cover all 23 discovered public projects with a named observed interaction or actual boundary. These later results supersede earlier automation limits only for their actual tested actions. Jev counts above exclude those separate Playwright flows. No complete implementation or universal mobile pass is inferred.

The separate [Givlah recording review](GIVLAH-VIDEO-OBSERVATIONS.md) inspects nine rendered timestamp samples at approximately 0:21, 0:41, 1:01, 1:21, 1:41, 2:01, 2:21, 2:41 and 3:01. Continuous playback, audio and every intervening transition remain unreviewed. This supplement does not change the frozen 77-run Jev count.
