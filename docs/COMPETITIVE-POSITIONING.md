# LivingShield: what to demonstrate

Prepared 21 October 2026 from the public visual benchmark, its two supplemental functional reports, and the verified local production build. These are concrete presentation choices, not an official ranking or a prediction of winning. The discovered repository list includes duplicate projects and unconfirmed candidates; it is not a count of entrant teams.

The strongest demonstration is the connected decision: **place → declared storm → local storage comparison → inspectable bank concept → reviewable brief**. Show the actual result for the chosen inputs and keep the bank concept's unvalidated performance separate from storage captured by the numerical model. The physical design and the storm comparison appearing together do not establish a combined flood benefit.

## Presentation sequence

Use Bandar Bukit Raja for the rehearsed example. Begin on the landing page, enter the guide, declare the storm, compute the baseline and finite local-storage case, and compare at one timestamp. Inspect the supplied GLB, change a bank dimension to show quantities reacting, then download the readable brief and full JSON. Leave the optional module rate blank unless a quotation exists. Use the actual guide attachment status rather than suggesting every step is automatically complete.

Keep nationwide Malaysian datasets available for follow-up questions. Do not overload the opening with every map layer or imply that three detailed pilot windows reconstruct all Malaysian townships. Explain the coarse historical DSM, omitted networks/inflows and need for calibration before presenting any grid depth.

## What public evidence does not establish

The 68-candidate public roster and attempted public-demo checks cannot prove coverage of private, unindexed or future entrants. A working sample interface does not validate telemetry, finance, clinical logic, physical performance or backend operations. The functional reports name their exact actions and limits; the source inventory labels maintainer claims separately. No uniqueness, award probability, calibrated flood prediction, installed-bank performance or complete commercial cost is established.
